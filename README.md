# Koda Roleplay — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Last Updated:** 17 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Koda Roleplay** ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, retains, and discloses information when providing Discord and FiveM integration features.

The Bot is operated by **SlaxWRLD** ("we", "us", or "our").

Koda Roleplay is a private bot intended for use within its designated Discord community and associated FiveM server environment.

We aim to process information only as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

## 2. Information We May Process

The information processed depends on the features enabled, the permissions granted, and the way the Bot is configured.

### 2.1 Discord Account and Server Information

The Bot may process:

- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Usernames, display names, and server nicknames.
- Role IDs and permission information needed for authorised functions.
- Channel IDs and message metadata.
- Information submitted through slash commands, buttons, forms, or other Bot interactions.
- Administrative and moderation actions associated with the Bot's operation.

### 2.2 Discord Message and Event Logs

Where Discord message logging is enabled and configured, the Bot may process relevant message information, which may include:

- Message content.
- Message authors' Discord IDs.
- Channel and server identifiers.
- Message timestamps.
- Relevant message edits or deletions, where supported by the configured logging feature.

The Bot's ability to access message content depends on its implementation, permissions, and applicable Discord platform requirements.

We do not claim to collect every message across Discord. Logging is limited to the functionality implemented and information accessible to the Bot.

### 2.3 FiveM Information

The Bot may process information from the associated FiveM server, including:

- In-game player names.
- Player identifiers made available to the integration.
- Player counts and server status.
- Discord and FiveM account-linking records, where enabled.
- Server events, administrative activity, and relevant gameplay logs.

FiveM identifiers may include platform-specific identifiers or other values made available by the server integration. Their exact type depends on the implementation.

### 2.4 Configuration and Technical Information

The Bot may store:

- Server configuration and feature settings.
- Configured channel and role IDs.
- Account-linking associations.
- Operational logs and error information.
- Data needed to maintain Bot functionality between restarts.

We do not intentionally collect information unrelated to the Bot's stated functionality.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be processed to:

- Operate the Bot and its FiveM integration.
- Display server status and player counts.
- Associate Discord accounts with FiveM identifiers when linking is enabled.
- Perform authorised administrative and moderation actions.
- Record configured server, message, and moderation events.
- Maintain server settings and configuration.
- Investigate errors, security issues, and misuse.
- Respond to support or privacy requests.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information as part of the Bot's operation or use information for unrelated advertising.

## 4. Where Information Is Stored

Koda Roleplay uses both databases and files to store information needed for its operation.

The Bot is hosted on a **Windows VPS**. Data may be stored in the associated database, configuration files, and operational logs.

The specific hosting provider, database technology, and physical location of stored data have not been identified in this policy. The operator should update this section if those details become known or change.

Access to stored information should be restricted to authorised individuals and services that require it for operation or maintenance.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Retention depends on the type of information and the feature involved:

- **Configuration data:** retained while needed to operate the Bot and its configured features.
- **Account-linking records:** retained while needed to maintain the relevant association or for another legitimate, disclosed purpose.
- **Message, moderation, and server logs:** retained while reasonably necessary for administration, security, troubleshooting, or other relevant operational purposes.
- **Technical logs:** retained while needed to diagnose problems and protect the service.
- **Support correspondence:** retained while needed to handle the request and maintain appropriate records.

We have not specified fixed retention periods for these categories. We will review retention and delete information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable law and legitimate security or recordkeeping requirements.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed where reasonably necessary for the Bot's operation, including:

- **Discord:** the Bot communicates with Discord's platform and APIs to provide its features.
- **VPS and infrastructure providers:** the provider hosting the Bot and associated storage may process or have technical access to information as part of providing infrastructure services.
- **FiveM integration:** information may be exchanged with the associated FiveM server to provide configured features.
- **Legal requirements:** information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process.

We do not identify a particular hosting or database provider in this policy because the provider has not been specified.

We do not authorise third parties to use Bot information for unrelated purposes. Any sharing must be consistent with the Bot's functionality, applicable platform policies, and applicable law.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Security measures should be appropriate to the data and environment, including restricting access to the Windows VPS, database, configuration files, and Bot credentials.

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot promise that unauthorised access or data loss will never occur.

## 8. Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

You may contact the operator to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of the Bot, subject to applicable law.

To submit a request, use the contact details in Section 12 and include enough information to identify the relevant Discord account, server, or data category.

We may need to verify your identity or authority before processing a request.

Where a request is valid and the information is no longer required for a permitted purpose, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise address the request. Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

## 9. Removing the Bot and Deleting Stored Data

Removing Koda Roleplay from a Discord server stops the Bot from operating in that server, subject to any remaining technical processes. **Removal does not necessarily delete information already stored in databases, files, backups, or logs.**

If you want information deleted, submit a separate request using the contact details below.

Where the Bot is permanently discontinued, the operator should delete stored information that is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Deletion from active storage may not immediately remove every copy from backups or retained technical records. Any such copies should be retained only as necessary and deleted through the applicable retention process.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to be used in a way that violates applicable minimum-age requirements or privacy laws.

If you believe that personal information has been collected unlawfully, contact the operator so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate steps taken.
